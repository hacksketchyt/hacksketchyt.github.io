HACK SKETCH

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy covers the Hack Sketch website and the Hack Sketch Pipeline application, an internal tool used only by the owner of the Hack Sketch YouTube channel to publish that channel's own videos.

Who uses the app

The app is not offered to the public. The only account that signs in to it is the Hack Sketch channel owner's Google account.

What Google data the app accesses

  • YouTube upload and management (youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl): to upload Hack Sketch videos, set their thumbnails, captions, schedule and playlist.
  • YouTube Analytics, read-only (yt-analytics.readonly): to read views, watch time, likes and subscribers for the channel's own videos, used to choose future video topics.

The app does not read, collect or store data about viewers, commenters or any other YouTube users, and it does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google service.

How data is stored and shared

The OAuth token and channel statistics are stored privately on a server controlled by the channel owner. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions. On revocation, the stored token and statistics are deleted from the server within 7 days.

Website

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics of its own. Hack pages embed videos with YouTube's privacy-enhanced player, and YouTube may store data on your device once you press play, as described in Google's privacy policy. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic request data as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

Questions: send a message via the Hack Sketch YouTube channel.
